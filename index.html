Privacy Policy

PC Master Control — Last Updated: October 26, 2023

PC Master Control ("we," "our," or "us") provides a remote management and monitoring application for your personal computer. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

a. Technical Data

To facilitate the connection between your Android device and your PC, the app may collect and process:

  • IP Addresses: To identify and connect to your PC on the local network.
  • System Statistics: CPU usage, GPU usage, RAM usage, storage details, and battery status from the connected PC.

b. Files and Media

  • File Transfer: The app allows you to browse and transfer files between your Android device and your connected PC. We do not store these files on our servers; they remain on your local devices.
  • Screen Mirroring: The app uses Android's Screen Capture API to mirror your Android screen to your PC. This data is transmitted directly over your local network and is not stored or shared by us.

2. Permissions Used

The app may request the following permissions to provide its features:

  • Internet / Network: Required to communicate with your PC.
  • Wi‑Fi State: Used for discovering PCs on your local network.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain screen mirroring and connection stability.
  • Storage / Media: Required for the File Transfer feature.

3. Data Usage and Security

  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.
  • Local Communication: Most data transmission occurs over your local network (LAN) and does not leave your private environment unless you have specifically configured your network for remote access.
  • No Data Retention: We do not collect or store any of your personal data on any external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that collect your data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
