1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to PCLink, developed by Beyond Edge. PCLink provides high-performance PC remote control over local Wi-Fi and standalone Universal Remote control for household appliances via IR Blaster and local network protocols.

Our Core Privacy Guarantee: PCLink operates on a strict 100% Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Local Area Network (LAN) architecture. We do not operate cloud databases, relay servers, or tracking telemetry. Your keystrokes, notifications, files, screen pixels, and remote commands NEVER leave your local network.

🔑

3. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or GPS locations are collected.
  • No Account Sign-in: No login, registration, or passwords required.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: Zero tracking of your button presses, sessions, or PC activities.
  • No Ad SDKs: 100% free of advertising banners, video ads, or marketing trackers.
📱

4. Universal Remote (IR Blaster & Local Wi-Fi)

The Universal Remote is 100% decoupled from the PC Master server. Commands sent to Smart TVs, Sun Direct DTH boxes, Fire TV sticks, ACs, or Fans go directly to the respective appliance without affecting PC audio or PC controls.

🖥️

5. Screen Mirroring & Media Projection

Screen mirroring requires explicit user consent on every session. Frames are encoded in-memory and sent directly over your private Wi-Fi network. No video files or screenshots are stored or transmitted outside.

🔔

6. Notification Sync Service

This optional feature allows you to mirror notifications to your PC tray over LAN while gaming. Notifications are never saved to disk, never sent to external servers, and discarded immediately after display.

🔒

7. Data Security & Local Network Safety

PCLink includes optional Security Token authentication to ensure only authorized devices on your Wi-Fi can connect to your PC. All preferences are saved strictly on your local device.

🚫

8. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertisements

PCLink contains zero third-party advertisement SDKs (no AdMob, Facebook Ads, Unity Ads) and zero commercial tracking libraries (no Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Flurry).

👶

9. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, PCLink is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR. It is safe for all age groups.

🗑️

10. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Rights

Since we do not store your data on any server, there is no data to retain or delete. You can clear local settings anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
